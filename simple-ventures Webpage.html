Our Team

Meet the leaders behind Simple Ventures

Bryan Porter

President
Bryan Porter is the President of Simple Ventures and co-founder of Simple Modern. Prior to launching Simple Ventures, Bryan served as Chief E-Commerce Officer of Simple Modern, where he was responsible for digital sales, including Amazon, DTC and international. Bryan has 14 years of experience in e-commerce, analytics, and growing startup businesses. Prior to starting Simple Modern in 2015, Bryan served as the Director of Analytics at QuiBids, and he graduated from the University of Oklahoma with a Bachelor of Business Administration degree in Energy Management.

Lee Graves

Chief Operating Officer
Lee Graves is the Chief Operating Officer at Simple Ventures. Lee has more than 20 years of experience in production management, engineering design and project management. Prior to joining Simple Ventures, Lee served as the Chief Manufacturing Officer for Simple Modern for 10 years, and as Director of Mining Operations for ACG Materials where he was responsible for nine facilities and more than $80 million in assets. Lee graduated from the University of Oklahoma with a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering and a Master of Business Administration.

About Simple Ventures

Simple Ventures is a family of brands committed to making a positive impact. We believe in building companies that serve our customers with excellence, support our employees with purpose, partner with integrity, and contribute meaningfully to our communities.

Our portfolio spans innovative consumer brands, each dedicated to delivering exceptional quality and value while staying true to our core mission of positive impact.

Each brand family is committed to donating 10% of all profits to charitable organizations in support of communities and causes around the world. For more information on the organizations we serve, please download our community impact report.
